open by a probe: #1/#2 → phase 2, #6 → phase 3, #9 → phase 4, #11 → phase 5,

## Most surprising finding

Bullet #3's own spec text ("specified ahead of a tracking plan") is wrong —
it *is* tracked, by `docs/plans/20260704-model-updates-l4-batched.md` Phase
BL8, which has simply never been started (`pending`). The four-plans framing
in the outcome's "Source" header undersells this: at least two of the seven
phase-mapped residues (#3, #11) are tracked by the *same* plan
(`20260704-model-updates-l4-batched.md`) under different phase names (BL8,
BL6), and BL6's "done" status could be misread as closing the broader
"no end-to-end run" divergence when it only ever closed the narrower
trace/bound-admission slice — a reminder that a plan's own Progress table
`done` marker answers "was this phase's scope completed," not "is the spec's
divergence bullet closed."
